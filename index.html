Where did they all go?

Since the 1960s, NASA has sent robots to explore the Moon, Mars and beyond. Most of them finished their jobs and went quiet. Some ran out of power. Some got stuck. Some were simply parked.

But they did not disappear. They are still out there right now. Let us go and meet them.

The Overachievers

Do you remember them?

Silent, but not gone

Each of these robots taught us something: how the Moon's soil feels, where water may once have flowed on Mars, whether a helicopter can fly on another world, and what lies between the stars. Future robots build on what they learned.

Abandoned? Maybe. Forgotten? Not while you remember them.
